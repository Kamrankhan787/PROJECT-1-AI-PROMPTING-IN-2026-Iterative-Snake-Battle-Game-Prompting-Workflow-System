# Step 3: Battle Prompt

## Human Prompt
```text
Now make it a battle: add computer-controlled snakes,
and when a snake dies its body turns into fruit
the others can eat.
```

## Prompt Engineering Context (2026 Methodology)
* **Goal**: Major rule change and mechanic transformation.
* **Key Concept**: "Change rules when necessary." Shifting from solo survival to dynamic competitive battle royale dynamics creates emergent gameplay and radically raises the fun factor.
* **Expected Output**:
  1. Multiple autonomous AI snakes navigating towards fruit.
  2. Fatal collision detection between snakes (head-to-body, head-to-head).
  3. Body segment conversion into high-value corpse fruits upon death.
